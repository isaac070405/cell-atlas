# Code of conduct

Cell Atlas is a teaching and reference project. This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) 2.1 (CC BY 4.0).

## Our pledge

Contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, language, or field of study.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, including with beginners.
- Give feedback on the work and not on the person, and accept feedback the same way.
- Welcome contributions in English and Traditional Chinese.
- Cite sources for scientific claims and correct errors openly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discriminatory jokes.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Trolling, or repeated disruption of issues and pull requests.
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional or academic setting.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a violation privately through GitHub private vulnerability reporting on the **Security** tab, or by contacting the repository owner, `@isaac070405`, on GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer aims to reply within 7 days.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban a contributor. The maintainer considers the context and the harm done before choosing a response.
