# Anatomical Planes 3D Model — Contents

Interactive three.js model of the human body (`Anatomical 3D Model.html`). Systems can be toggled, parts searched and selected, layers peeled, regions dissected, and any plane cut as a live cross-section. Exports OBJ + MTL and GLB.

## Interface

- **Sidebar sections:** Find a part · Systems · Heart, lungs and diaphragm · Brain and spine views · Body layers, skin to bone · Renal and reproductive · Upper limb · Lower limb · Peel layers · Dissect
- **Preset views:** Heart view · Thorax · Lateral · Medial · Inferior · Spine · Reset · Kidneys · Kidneys from behind · Bladder and pelvis · Male reproductive · Male pelvis from the side · Ovaries and uterus close-up (female) · Breast (female) · Breast from the side (female) · Shoulder and arm · Forearm and hand · Hip and thigh · Leg and foot
- **Cross-section panel:** transverse / coronal / sagittal cut, slider position, zoom − / + / Fit, drag to pan, scroll to zoom, minimise (−), enlarge (⤢), close (×), optional cutting plane shown in the model, colour key per system.

### Body layers (skin to bone)

`layers.html` opens the explorer on the layers panel. A depth slider peels 14 layers from the outside in, and each layer has its own toggle.

1. Skin · 2. Superficial veins · 3. Investing fascia · 4. Superficial muscles · 5. Second muscle group · 6. Intermediate muscles · 7. Deep muscles · 8. Deepest muscles · 9. Visceral coverings · 10. Anterior organs · 11. Deep organs · 12. Deep nerves and vessels · 13. Capsules and ligaments · 14. Bones and cartilage

`layers.js` assigns each mesh to a layer by system and label (`layerOf`), following the reference list "Anatomical Structures Reordered by Layers". Structures the list does not name are placed by depth, so new structures land in a layer by name.

### Explode mode and selection tab

- **Modes:** Assemble anatomy keeps the body together. Explode anatomy pulls bones, muscles, brain and nerves, arteries, veins, organs and airway, and lymphatics into seven columns on a dark background.
- **Regions:** Whole body, Torso and pelvis, Head and neck, Upper extremity, Lower extremity. Each column button opens that system alone, with a back button.
- **Selection tab:** clicking a structure opens a tab with a Surrounding anatomy slider (100% filled to 0% only the selection), Center selected structure, Show selected structure only (30% visible), and Hide selected structure. Hidden structures can be restored with Undo in the Dissect section.

## Modelling notes

- Organ sizes run about 60–80% of textbook measurements so neighbouring structures fit without overlap.
- Vessels that join major trunks (renal, testicular, gut arteries) belong to Circulatory so connections stay intact when systems are hidden.
- Translucent walls (kidney cortex, stomach, bladder, prostate, scrotum) expose internal structure; subsurface effects are approximated with sheen and clearcoat.
- Fine surface textures (gastric pits, filiform papillae, skin micro-rugae) are omitted because textures don't survive OBJ export.
- A small disclaimer under the page lede links to `known-limitations.html`, which lists the areas that are approximate, thin or missing.
- A row of six camera buttons (Anterior, Posterior, Right, Left, Superior, Inferior) swings the camera around the current target at the current distance. Right and left are the figure's own.
- Clicking a structure flies the camera to frame it (switch off with the checkbox under the view buttons). Hovering a structure outlines it in amber.
- Reproductive system has male and female variants, switched with the Sex control; penis shown flaccid.
- The female body mesh (`uploads/Female_Muscular_BaseMesh`) is not in the repository. Without it the generic body surface stays on, minus the male genitalia, and the female structures still show.
- The menstrual cycle slider (day 1 to 28) changes endometrial thickness, the dominant follicle on the right ovary, the released oocyte on day 14 and the corpus luteum in the luteal phase.

## Body part views (panel groups 1 to 6)

- The side panel lists body-part views in six groups: brain and spine, head, neck, thoracic and abdominal cavity, upper limb, lower limb. Each button shows one set of structures and flies the camera to them. Press the active button again, or Reset, to bring everything back.
- Head: eye, outer, middle, inner and whole ear, nasal cavity, oral cavity, teeth, oral cavity with teeth, skull and teeth. Nasal, oral and pharynx views cut the body in the midline and show the right half from the left.
- Single tooth: a magnified canine cut lengthwise (enamel, dentin and its tubules, pulp chamber and root canal, cementum, periodontal ligament, alveolar bone, gingiva, apical foramen, nerve, artery and vein), beside the figure like the alveolar unit.
- Single alveolar unit: terminal bronchiole to alveolar sac, one capillary network per alveolus (blue arriving, red leaving), pores of Kohn, a cutaway wall with type I and II pneumocytes and a macrophage, and a blood-air barrier inset.
- Thoracic and abdominal cavity: heart (eight great vessels, chambers and valves, coronary arteries and veins), lungs and diaphragm, airway tubes (trachea, bronchus, bronchi, larynx, pharynx, oropharynx, nasopharynx, nasal cavity), renal, reproductive, digestive (oesophagus, stomach, small and large intestine, liver with gallbladder and bile duct, pancreas with its duct, all digestive), tonsils, lymphatic system.
- Lymphatic system: a new system with node groups of the head and neck, thorax, abdomen, pelvis and limbs, and the vessels, trunks and ducts that join them. The lymphatic views also gather the thoracic duct, cisterna chyli, tonsils, spleen, thymus, Peyer's patches and appendix from the systems that hold them.
- Rebuilt: the nasal cavity (scrolled conchae, meatuses, olfactory region, vestibule), the larynx (thyroid, cricoid, arytenoid and epiglottic cartilages, true and false vocal folds) and the pharynx, now split into nasopharynx, oropharynx and laryngopharynx.
- Rebuilt oral cavity and tonsils: upper and lower lip, a vestibule lined by labial and buccal mucosa, alveolar mucosa and labial frenula; a vaulted hard palate with a median raphe, incisive papilla and rugae; a soft palate with its aponeurosis and uvula; tensor and levator veli palatini, palatoglossus, palatopharyngeus, salpingopharyngeus and musculus uvulae; faucial arches around a crypted palatine tonsil in each tonsillar fossa (capsule, supratonsillar fossa, triangular and semilunar folds); the pharyngeal tonsil with its bursa, the tubal tonsils and tubal openings, and the lingual tonsil, which together make Waldeyer's ring. The oropharynx now opens at the faucial isthmus. The floor of the mouth has its mucosa, sublingual folds and plicae fimbriatae; the tongue gains intrinsic muscles (superior and inferior longitudinal, transverse, vertical), a median sulcus, foliate papillae and glossoepiglottic folds with the vallecula. Parotid, submandibular and sublingual glands have ducts that open at the parotid papilla and the sublingual caruncle.
- Rebuilt neck: sternocleidomastoid in sternal and clavicular heads; omohyoid as superior belly, intermediate tendon and inferior belly; the three scalenes; longus colli (vertical, superior and inferior oblique parts), longus capitis, rectus capitis anterior and lateralis; splenius cervicis and the nuchal ligament; a thyroid gland whose lobes wrap the trachea, with an isthmus over rings 2 to 4, a pyramidal lobe and four flat parathyroid glands; the carotid sinus and body, the ascending pharyngeal artery; and the lesser occipital, great auricular, transverse cervical and supraclavicular nerves from the cervical plexus. The phrenic nerve now runs on the front of the anterior scalene.
- New body part views: Salivary glands (head), Deep neck muscles and Thyroid and parathyroid (neck). The Tonsils view now shows all of Waldeyer's ring, and the Oropharynx, Nasopharynx and Oral cavity views include the new structures.
